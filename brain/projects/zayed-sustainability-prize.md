## Problem Statement

- Soil infertility and structural decay
- Water scarcity and surface evaporation (up to 70% loss in conventional irrigation)
- Food insecurity, famine, malnutrition (2,200 Ugandans dying of hunger; 24%+ children under 5 malnourished)
- Climate change / desertification in Northern Uganda and semi-arid regions

---

## Technical Solution

### The Soil Skeleton
- Hexagonal grid of underground pipes + "nerve"-like probe structures
- Capacitive soil moisture sensors detect per-zone water content
- ESP32 microcontroller reads sensors and opens valves automatically
- Water + soluble phosphate mixed via Venturi Injector
- Carbon-coated pipes to prevent soil toxicity
- Probes create permanent aeration tunnels

### The Hygro-Electric Conditioning Tower
- Modified wind turbine generates electricity AND drives high-pressure pump
- Releases water mist carried by wind to increase local humidity
- Boosts rainfall formation probability without cloud dependency

### Technology Used
- ESP32 cores / motherboards
- Capacitive soil moisture sensors
- Water filtration system
- Composting systems
- Digital platforms and databases
- Solar panels + pumping systems
- Batteries and motors
